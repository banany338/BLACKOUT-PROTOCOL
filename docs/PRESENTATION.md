## Three-minute speaking script

### 1. The question (0:00–0:20)

Your main account is gone. Can your team recover? BLACKOUT PROTOCOL helps small teams prepare for that situation before it becomes an emergency.

### 2. The problem (0:20–0:42)

A club's files, website and social accounts can depend on the same email or person. Recovery can depend on that missing account too. Having a backup is not enough if the backup needs the account it should recover.

### 3. Build the map (0:42–1:05)

Start with one service and its owner. Record what access needs and how the owner could get back in. Add the other services. The app connects these arrangements to the work your team needs to do.

### 4. Check a loss (1:05–1:30)

Choose an unavailable account, phone or backup. The same map shows the consequences. In this example, losing email and its backup leaves only one of three activities working. Everything happens in the sandbox. Real accounts stay unchanged.

### 5. Prepare the improvement (1:30–1:55)

Evaluate an independent fallback. Save it as a separate proposal so a possible improvement is not mistaken for your current setup. Assign the preparation to its owner, who still needs to configure and check the real method.

### 6. Check with the owners (1:55–2:20)

The team can check the same map together. The host reviews what is shared, approves people and assigns account responsibilities. Missing prerequisites block simulated steps. One owner's recovery can make another owner's next step possible.

### 7. Keep the result (2:20–2:45)

Take away a readable recovery reference, an encrypted backup and preparation tasks with owners. The implementation passes 41 unit and integration tests and nine browser journeys. Real provider recovery and production security still need validation.

### 8. Close (2:45–3:00)

BLACKOUT turns a dependency the team might overlook into a concrete recovery arrangement someone can prepare. Find the dead end. Prepare the way back.
